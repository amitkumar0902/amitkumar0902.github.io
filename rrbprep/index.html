RRB Success Tips

Focus on Professional Ability

70 marks from nursing subjects. Master Med-Surg, OBG, Community, and Pharmacology first.

Time Management

54 seconds per question. Don't spend more than 90 seconds on any single question.

Negative Marking Strategy

1/3 mark deducted per wrong answer. Skip if less than 75% confident.

Previous Year Pattern

40–50% questions repeat from previous years. Solve 2019–2025 papers thoroughly.

Section Priority

Professional Ability first (60 min), then Science (5 min), GK (10 min), Math+Reasoning (10 min), Review (5 min).

Exam Day Prep

Reach center 1 hour early, carry RRB admit card + valid photo ID, no electronic devices.
