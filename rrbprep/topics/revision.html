Flashcards & Mnemonics for All Topics

Mnemonics

Nursing Process

  • ADPIE — Assessment, Diagnosis, Planning, Implementation, Evaluation
  • ABC — Airway, Breathing, Circulation (emergency)

Vital Signs (Normal Adult)

  • BP: 90/60 – 120/80 mmHg
  • Pulse: 60–100/min; Resp: 12–20/min
  • Temp: 98.6°F (37°C); SpO2: 95–100%

BMW Colors (2016 Rules)

  • Yellow — Infectious, anatomical, soiled
  • Red — Recyclable contaminated
  • White/Translucent — Sharps

Drug Antidotes

Common Antidotes

  • Heparin → Protamine sulfate
  • Warfarin → Vitamin K
  • Opioids → Naloxone
  • Paracetamol → N-acetylcysteine
  • Anaphylaxis → Epinephrine (first line)

Normal Lab Values (Quick)

Serum

  • Potassium: 3.5–5.0 mEq/L
  • Sodium: 136–145 mEq/L
  • Fasting glucose: 70–100 mg/dL
  • Blood pH: 7.35–7.45

Immunization (UIP) at a Glance

At Birth

  • BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B (1st dose)

6, 10, 14 weeks

  • OPV, DPT, Hepatitis B (2nd & 3rd), Hib, PCV, Rota (as per schedule)

9–12 months

  • Measles/MMR, Vitamin A (1st dose)
