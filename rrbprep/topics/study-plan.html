AIR 1-30 Study Plan

Week-by-Week Strategy for Top Rank

Score Target for AIR 1-30

Section-wise Target

  • Professional Ability: 65-68 / 70 (93-97%)
  • General Awareness: 8-9 / 10
  • Arithmetic + Reasoning: 8-9 / 10
  • General Science: 9-10 / 10
  • Total: 90-95 / 100

Attempt Strategy

  • Attempt 95-98 questions
  • Skip 2-5 when <75% confident
  • 1/3 negative marking: avoid blind guessing

Key Rule

  • Never guess randomly (expected value ≈ 0 with 4 options)
  • Eliminate wrong options first
  • Mark and review doubtful ones in last 5 min

6-Week Preparation Plan

Week 1-2: Professional Ability Core

  • Day 1-3: Anatomy & Physiology + Nutrition (60 Qs)
  • Day 4-6: Nursing Foundations + Pharmacology (75 Qs)
  • Day 7-9: Medical-Surgical Part 1 (CVS, Respiratory, GI)
  • Day 10-12: Medical-Surgical Part 2 (Renal, Endocrine, Emergency)
  • Day 13-14: Obstetric & Midwifery (35 Qs)

Week 3-4: Extended + Community

  • Day 15-17: Community Health + Environmental Health
  • Day 18-19: Pediatric Nursing
  • Day 20-21: Psychiatric Nursing
  • Day 22-23: Microbiology
  • Day 24-25: Pathology, Genetics, Psychology, Sociology
  • Day 26-28: Research & Management + Revision Week 1-2

Week 5-6: General + Mock + Revision

  • Day 29-30: General Science
  • Day 31-32: General Awareness
  • Day 33-34: Arithmetic & Reasoning
  • Day 35: Full Mock Test 1 (100 Q, 90 min)
  • Day 36-37: Previous year papers (2019-2025)
  • Day 38-39: Weak area revision
  • Day 40: Full Mock Test 2
  • Day 41: Mnemonics, formulas, normal values
  • Day 42 (Exam eve): Light revision, checklist, sleep early

Daily Schedule Template

Morning

  • 6:00 - 8:00 AM: High-yield nursing topics (fresh mind)
  • 8:00 - 9:00 AM: Break + light exercise
  • 9:00 - 12:00 PM: Practice questions + explanation review

Afternoon & Evening

  • 12:00 - 2:00 PM: Lunch + rest
  • 2:00 - 4:00 PM: General sections (GK, Science, Math)
  • 4:00 - 5:00 PM: Break
  • 5:00 - 7:00 PM: Previous year paper solving
  • 7:00 - 8:00 PM: Quick revision / mnemonics
  • 8:00 PM onwards: Dinner, relaxation, 7-8 hours sleep

Accuracy Tips for AIR 1-30

  • Never guess randomly (expected value with 4 options ≈ 0)
  • Master elimination—removing 1 wrong option improves expected value
  • Read all 4 options before answering
  • Mark and skip difficult; return in review time
  • Don't change answers unless you find a clear reason
