Effective: [TODO: publish date] · NurseDrill (nursedrill.com), operated by [TODO confirm legal name: Amit Amarwanshi], India.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — your email and name (from Google sign-in or the signup form).
  • Study data — your practice progress, mock scores, streaks, and settings, so they sync across your devices.
  • Device info — basic device/browser identifiers and last-seen times, used only to show you your signed-in devices. We don't limit devices.
  • Payment status — your plan and its validity date, plus the payment reference our payment partner sends us. We never receive or store your card, UPI, or bank details — payment happens entirely on the payment partner's secure page.
  • Reports you send — question-error reports and support emails.

2. What we don't collect

No location, no contacts, no files, no advertising identifiers. We don't sell or rent personal data to anyone, ever.

3. How we use it

To run the service: sign you in, sync progress, unlock what you paid for, answer support, fix reported errors, and understand aggregate usage so the product improves.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to measure aggregate usage — pages, funnels, feature use. This involves cookies/identifiers processed by Google. You can block analytics with standard browser tools without affecting the product.

5. Where data lives (processors)

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) in the Mumbai (asia-south1) region, and processed by Google Analytics. Payments are processed by [Razorpay / Cashfree — TODO: final gateway]. These providers process data for us under their own security and privacy commitments.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account (account page → Delete my account) permanently removes your authentication record, study data, and device info. Payment audit records are retained as required for accounting and tax law.

7. Your rights and choices

You can access your data in-product, export progress, correct your details, and delete your account yourself. For anything else — including grievances under Indian law — write to support@nursedrill.com; the grievance process on the Contact page applies (acknowledgement within 48 hours, resolution within 30 days).

8. Security

Access to content and your data is enforced server-side with per-user security rules; connections are HTTPS; we hold no payment instruments. No system is perfectly secure, but the attack surface is deliberately tiny.

9. Age

NurseDrill is intended for users 18 and over (the exams it prepares you for require it).

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above always reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced on the site.
