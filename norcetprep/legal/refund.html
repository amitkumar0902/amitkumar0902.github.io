Refund & Cancellation Policy

The short version: 7 days, no questions asked, one refund per account. And because plans never auto-renew, there is nothing you ever need to "cancel".

Refunds

If you buy a plan and it isn't for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of purchase — no reasons needed. Each account can use this once. Launch-price and discounted purchases have exactly the same refund rights.

How to request one

Use the "Request a refund" option on your account page, or email support@nursedrill.com from your account email with your payment reference.

Timelines

We initiate the refund within 48 hours of your request. The money reaches your original payment method in 5–7 business days (the payment network's timeline, not ours). Premium access ends when the refund is initiated.

Cancellation

There is no auto-renewal and no recurring charge at NurseDrill — a plan is a single payment for a fixed period, and access simply ends when the period does. We'll remind you before expiry; renewing is entirely your choice. So there is never a subscription to cancel, a mandate to revoke, or a trap to escape.

After the 7-day window

Beyond 7 days, payments are non-refundable. Everything the plan includes is listed openly on the pricing page, and free samples let you judge the quality before paying — we'd rather you try before you buy.

Questions

support@nursedrill.com — payment and access issues are handled same-day. Full contact details and the grievance process are on the Contact page.
