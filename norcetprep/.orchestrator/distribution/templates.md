## Rules these templates follow

- **Brand is NurseDrill**, always. Never build links or profile names under
  "norcetprep" — both domains were registered by someone else on 28 May 2026.
  "NORCET" stays in page titles and copy because that is what people search
  for; it is never the brand.
- **Link to free value, not to pricing.** The funnel converts on-product: send
  people to a free bank or the daily quiz.
- **Every post carries** "Not affiliated with or endorsed by AIIMS."
- **No dark patterns**: no countdowns, no fake scarcity, no invented faculty,
  no rank or selection promises. If a tactic needs one, the tactic is wrong.
- **No spam**: one relevant post per group, answering the question actually
  asked. Nothing here is worth a ban.

## Quora / forum answer (outline)

1. Answer the question that was actually asked, in full, before linking
   anything. If the honest answer does not involve us, do not link.
2. One paragraph of real substance — the topics that carry the marks, the
   common traps, what to revise.
3. One descriptive link: "free NORCET {topic} MCQs with cited explanations"
   → {URL}
4. Say plainly: cross-check against the official AIIMS notification and
   standard textbooks; memory-based recall sets are for pattern, not truth.
5. Disclose: "I build NurseDrill." Never post as an anonymous happy user.
