Hand Hygiene — WHO 5 Moments

WHO 5 Moments:
  1. BEFORE touching a patient
  2. BEFORE clean / aseptic procedure
  3. AFTER body-fluid exposure
  4. AFTER touching a patient
  5. AFTER touching patient surroundings

Techniques & Duration

  • Routine handwash: Minimum 20 seconds with soap & water
  • Alcohol-based hand rub (ABHR): 20–30 seconds · rub until dry
  • Surgical scrub: 2–6 minutes
  • Steps: 6-step technique (palm-to-palm, back of hand, interlaced fingers, back of fingers, thumbs, fingertips)

ABHR Limitations — Use SOAP & WATER when:

  • Hands visibly soiled
  • C. difficile exposure (spore-former — not killed by alcohol)
  • Norovirus / Bacillus anthracis exposure
  • Before eating
  • After restroom

Biomedical Waste Management — BMW Rules 2016

ColorWaste TypeTreatment
Yellow Anatomical / pathological waste, soiled dressings, expired medicines, chemical waste, microbiological / lab waste, discarded linen with fluids, chemo waste Incineration / deep burial / plasma pyrolysis
Red Contaminated recyclable waste — IV tubing, IV sets, urine bags, catheters, syringes (without needle), gloves Autoclaving / microwaving / hydroclaving → recycling
White (translucent puncture-proof) Sharps — needles, scalpel blades, suture needles, glass ampoules Autoclave / dry heat sterilization → shredding → final disposal
Blue (puncture-proof cardboard) Broken glassware, metallic body implants Disinfection → washing → recycling

Key Principles

  • Segregation at point of generation is the MOST important step
  • Fill bags up to ¾ (75%) capacity only — never overfill
  • Bar-coding of bags for tracking
  • Pre-treatment of lab / microbiological waste before disposal
  • Needle destroyers / hub cutters mandatory at point of use

Venturi Mask — Color Coding & FiO₂

ColorFiO₂Recommended Flow (L/min)
Blue24%2–4
White28%4–6
Yellow35%8
Red40%8–12
Green60%12–15
Memory trick: "Blue-24, White-28, Yellow-35, Red-40, Green-60" — arranged lowest to highest concentration.

Venturi Mechanism & Indication

  • Mechanism: Air entrainment (Bernoulli principle) — delivers precise FiO₂
  • Best for: COPD patients where controlled low FiO₂ is essential
  • Independent of patient's tidal volume / breathing pattern
