Demographic Indicators — DFR, GFR, TFR

  • CBR (Crude Birth Rate) = Live births / mid-year population × 1000
  • GFR (General Fertility Rate) = Live births / Women aged 15–49 × 1000
  • TFR (Total Fertility Rate) India (current ~2.0) — replacement level ≈ 2.1
  • IMR — Infant deaths <1 yr / 1000 live births
  • MMR — Maternal deaths / 100,000 live births
  • DFR — Death-to-Fertility ratio / Demographic indicator (use the stem context)
Key: Water contamination indicator = Coliform (fecal). Common cause of mortality in India = TB. Most common female cancer India = Breast CA. Screening for cervical CA = VIA with acetic acid. (NORCET 9 PYQs)

Family Planning — Logos & Methods

Universal Symbol

Red inverted triangle = universal Family Planning symbol in India.

Methods Overview

  • Barrier: Condom, diaphragm
  • Hormonal: OCP, Mala-N/Mala-D, Antara (DMPA — injection quarterly), Chhaya (non-hormonal Centchroman)
  • IUD: Copper-T 380A (10 yrs), Cu-375 (5 yrs), Post-partum IUCD (PPIUCD)
  • Emergency: Ezy-Pill (Levonorgestrel 1.5 mg within 72h)
  • Sterilization: Vasectomy (NSV), Tubectomy (laparoscopic / mini-lap / postpartum)

HPV Vaccination — India 2026 (LATEST)

India launched nationwide HPV vaccination: February 28, 2026
  • Target: Girls who have completed 14 years of age
  • Schedule: SINGLE DOSE (WHO 2022 recommendation)
  • Vaccine: Gardasil-4 (quadrivalent — HPV types 6, 11, 16, 18)
  • Dose: 0.5 mL intramuscular in left upper arm (deltoid)
  • Digital Platform: U-WIN registry
  • Free & voluntary; parental consent required
  • PLWH (immunocompromised): Minimum 2 doses recommended
  • Cervavac — India's indigenous quadrivalent HPV vaccine (Serum Institute)

Cervical Cancer Key Facts

  • 2nd most common cancer in women in India
  • HPV types 16 & 18 cause ~70% of cervical cancer
  • Screening: VIA (Visual Inspection with Acetic acid) — community setting
  • Pap smear / HPV DNA testing — higher level
