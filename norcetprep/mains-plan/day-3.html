Labour Drugs & Eclampsia

Magnesium Sulfate (MgSO₄) — Drug of Choice for Eclampsia

  • Action: Anticonvulsant (NOT primarily antihypertensive)
  • Therapeutic level: 4 – 7 mEq/L
  • Loading dose: 4–6 g IV over 15–20 min, then 1–2 g/hr infusion
MgSO₄ Toxicity — Monitor BEFORE each dose:
  1. Deep tendon reflexes — loss of DTR is the earliest sign
  2. Respiratory rate — hold if RR <12/min
  3. Urine output — must be ≥30 mL/hr (kidney excretes MgSO₄)
  4. Serum magnesium — every 4–6 hours
Antidote: Calcium Gluconate 10% – 10 mL IV

Pre-eclampsia & HELLP

  • Pre-eclampsia diagnosis: BP ≥140/90 + proteinuria after 20 weeks
  • Severe features: BP ≥160/110, headache, visual changes, epigastric pain, ↑ LFTs, ↓ platelets, pulmonary edema
  • HELLP: Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes, Low Platelets
  • Only cure: Delivery of the fetus

3rd Stage of Labour — NEW Guidelines (AMTSL Update)

Modern Active Management of 3rd Stage (AMTSL)

  • Prophylactic oxytocin 10 IU IM/IV — MOST critical component (given within 1 min of delivery)
  • Delayed cord clamping: 1–3 minutes after delivery (replaces early clamping unless resuscitation required)
  • Controlled cord traction — optional; only by trained personnel
  • Uterine massage — NOT routinely recommended if oxytocin given (WHO 2018/2024 update)

New PPH Definitions (FIGO / WHO)

PPH diagnosed if: Blood loss >300 mL OR pulse >100 bpm OR SpO₂ <93% OR symptoms of hypovolemia.

Modern Quantitative / Emergency Tools

  • BRASS-V / V-drape — quantitative blood-loss measurement drape (NORCET 9 PYQ)
  • NASG (Non-Pneumatic Anti-Shock Garment) — used for severe PPH to redirect blood to vital organs during transfer
  • First-line PPH management: Uterine massage + IV oxytocin (NORCET 9 answer)
  • Second-line: Tranexamic acid + Methergine / Carboprost / Misoprostol
  • Mechanical: Bimanual compression, intrauterine balloon tamponade (Bakri balloon)

Classic Presentation

  • Uterus larger than gestational age (dates)
  • Vaginal bleeding with grape-like clusters
  • Hyperemesis gravidarum (from very high hCG)
  • PIH before 20 weeks (pathognomonic)
  • Theca lutein cysts on ovaries
  • No fetal heart tones

Management & Follow-up

  • Treatment: Suction evacuation (D&C)
  • Follow-up hCG: Weekly until negative × 3 consecutive, then monthly × 6–12 months (NORCET 9 PYQ: weekly β-hCG until 3 consecutive negative)
  • Avoid pregnancy for 12 months (effective contraception — avoid IUDs)
  • Monitor for choriocarcinoma — rising hCG → chemotherapy with Methotrexate
