🗺️ Site Map — What is each page for?

A quick plain-English tour, in case you're new here. Start at the top and work down.

1. Where do I start each day?

  • 13-Day Plan (this page) — your home base. Tells you which day of the 13-day schedule you're on and links to that day's notes + practice.
  • Day notes (Day 1 – Day 13) — short, scenario-first study notes for the day. Read this first, then do the practice.
  • Day practice sets — a small batch of MCQs for that day's topics. Do these right after the notes.

2. Study material — go deeper on any topic

  • Syllabus checklist — every one of the 610 official NORCET topics as a tickable list. "Critical 🔥" badges show the most-asked topics. Sort by "Critical first" to pick what to revise.
  • Scenario-first notes — the full notes library grouped into 13 subjects (Midwifery, OBG, Surgery, Foundation, CHN, Medicine, ENT, Pediatrics, Pharmacology, Mental Health, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Anatomy). Click a topic, read the clinical scenario + nursing priority, move on.
  • 🏆 Topper frequency analysis — which exact topics have repeated across NORCET 1.0–9.0. If you're short on time, revise these first.
  • Watch playlist — curated YouTube videos, per topic, if you prefer watching over reading.

3. Practice — solve MCQs

  • Extra Questions (the "bank") — the full MCQ library (~1,300 questions). Filter by subject, day, source, difficulty, or question type. Use "Try" mode to attempt first, "Browse" to read with answers open.
  • PYQ Vault — memory-based previous-year recalls from NORCET 6 / 7 / 8 / 9 Mains, organised by subject. Non-negotiable reading.
  • Drug calculation drill — focused reps on IV rates, dosages, drops/min. The easiest marks to lose if you skip practice.
  • Scenario drill — short clinical vignettes for rapid decision practice.
  • Flashcards — 7 high-yield decks (Lochia, PPH, etc.) for quick revision on the go.

4. Mocks — full-length timed exams

  • All mocks (12 total) — 10 full-length NORCET Mains simulations (160 Q / 180 min each), plus a PYQ-only warm-up mock and the NORCET 9 Mains 2025 recall set (121 Q, memory-based — reworded from candidate recall, not a reproduction of the paper). Every mock has +1 for correct, −1/3 for wrong.
  • NORCET 9 Mains 2025 replay — the 2025 paper, timed. Save this for the day before your exam as a dress rehearsal.
  • PYQ-Only mock — low-stakes warm-up made from verified recalls. Good for Day 0 or a confidence check.
  • Review — re-see wrong answers from every mock/practice, with the option's explanation. This is where the rank actually comes from.

5. Track progress + settings

  • Progress dashboard — your scores, subject-wise accuracy, time per question, streaks.
  • Settings — theme, font size, sign-in to sync across devices.
  • Cheatsheets — one-page summaries for exam-eve skim. Use on Day 13 only.

Suggested daily flow (2–3 hours)

  1. Open the 13-Day Plan → see today's day.
  2. Read that day's Notes (~45 min).
  3. Do that day's Practice set (~30 min).
  4. Open Review — re-read the explanations for anything you got wrong (~20 min).
  5. Optional: 10 minutes of drug calc drill or flashcards before bed.
  6. On Days 11–13, add one full mock + post-mortem per day.

Tip: every page has a top navigation bar — Home, Today, Practice, Mocks, Extra Qs, Flashcards, Progress, PYQs — so you can jump anywhere without coming back here.
