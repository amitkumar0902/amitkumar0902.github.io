Top-Rank Strategy at a Glance

  • Attempt: 150–155 questions (skip only when confidence < 60%)
  • Target accuracy: 90%+ on attempted questions
  • Pace: ~67 seconds per question; time-check every 40 questions
  • Read stems twice — circle EXCEPT, NOT, FIRST, BEST, PRIORITY
  • 1:1 Rule: Every hour of theory is matched with an hour of MCQ practice
  • 1:4 Rule on PYQs: Verify the correct answer + research all 3 wrong options
