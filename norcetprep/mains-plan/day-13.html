The One-Page Cheat Sheets

1. ABG Values

  • pH 7.35–7.45
  • PaCO₂ 35–45
  • HCO₃ 22–26
  • PaO₂ 80–100
  • ROME: Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal

2. ECG Essentials

  • PR 0.12–0.20 · QRS 0.06–0.10
  • 1°AV: PR >0.20
  • 2° Type I: PR lengthens → drop
  • 2° Type II: sudden drop
  • 3°: no relation (complete)
  • VT: wide, regular; VF: chaotic
  • WPW: short PR + delta wave

3. Burns

  • Rule of 9s · Parkland 4 mL × kg × %TBSA
  • 50% first 8h · 50% next 16h
  • Fluid: Ringer's Lactate
  • Airway priority · silver sulfadiazine

4. ET Tube

  • Uncuffed: (Age/4) + 4
  • Cuffed: (Age/4) + 3.5
  • Depth: ETT × 3
  • Adult F: 7.0–7.5 · M: 8.0–8.5

5. Drug Calc Formulas

  • (Desired / Have) × Vehicle
  • gtt/min = (Vol × factor) / min
  • Microdrip (60) → gtt/min = mL/hr
  • KCl max: peripheral 10, central 20 mEq/hr

6. IV Fluids

  • Vomiting → NS (alkalosis)
  • Burns, diarrhea → RL
  • Hypo-Na 120 → 3% saline
  • Hypo-Na 132 → 0.9% NS
  • TPN off → D10W

7. OBG Essentials

  • Eclampsia → MgSO₄ (antidote Ca-gluconate)
  • Previa = painless · Abruption = painful
  • Delayed cord clamp 1–3 min
  • PPH >300 mL (new)
  • Molar: weekly hCG × 3 normal

8. APGAR / NRP

  • Score 1 & 5 min
  • Pulse most important
  • NRP 3:1 compressions
  • SpO₂ @5 min: 80–85%
  • Bag-mask flow 15 L/min

9. Milestones

  • 2m smile · 4m roll · 6m sit
  • 9m crawl · 12m walk
  • 3y: tricycle, circle
  • 4y: hop, square
  • 5y: skip, triangle

10. Peds Disorders

  • TEF: 3 C's (cough, choke, cyano)
  • Croup: barking / steeple
  • Epiglottitis: drool / thumb
  • G6PD: avoid sulfa, fava, aspirin
  • Sickle: hydrate, warm, O₂
  • Hemophilia: no IM, no aspirin

11. Venturi Colors

  • Blue 24% · White 28%
  • Yellow 35% · Red 40% · Green 60%
  • Best for COPD — precise FiO₂

12. BMW 2016

  • Yellow: anatomical / incineration
  • Red: recyclable / autoclave
  • White: sharps
  • Blue: glass / metal
  • 75% fill limit · segregation at source

13. Hand Hygiene

  • WHO 5 moments
  • 20 sec routine · 2–6 min surgical
  • Soap + water for C.diff, norovirus

14. National Programs

  • ICDS: 0–6 yrs · MoWCD · Anganwadi
  • Polio-free India since 2011
  • JSY: institutional delivery
  • PMJAY: ₹5 lakh cover
  • NTEP RIPE drugs

15. HPV 2026

  • Launch: 28 Feb 2026
  • Girls ≥14 yr · single dose
  • Gardasil-4 / Cervavac
  • U-WIN platform

16. Infections

  • Candida: cottage cheese
  • BV: thin grey, fishy, clue cells
  • Trich: yellow-green frothy
  • CMV: hearing loss · toxo: cat litter

17. TPN / Central Line

  • Central only · never bolus
  • If off → D10W
  • Air embolism: L-Trendelenburg
  • CLABSI bundle: hand hygiene + max barrier + CHG

18. Cardiac Codes

  • BLS 30:2 · depth 2–2.4″
  • VF/pulseless VT: defib + CPR
  • Epi 1 mg every 3–5 min
  • Asystole: no defib
  • Bradycardia: atropine 1 mg

19. Priority Rules

  • ABCs always first
  • Maslow physiologic priority
  • New > expected · Unstable > stable
  • RN cannot delegate: assess, teach, plan, eval

20. Psychiatry Quick

  • Borderline → DBT
  • Bipolar I → mania
  • MAOI → avoid tyramine
  • EPS (haloperidol) → Trihexyphenidyl
  • Alcohol dep → Disulfiram / aversion
