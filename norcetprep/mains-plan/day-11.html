Full-Length Mock Test 1

Exam Simulation Rules — No Shortcuts

  • Morning slot (9 AM – 12 PM) matches likely exam timing
  • Phone OFF · bathroom only before start · water nearby
  • 160 questions / 180 minutes
  • Use -1/3 negative marking
  • No textbooks, no notes, no internet
  • Use scratch paper provided, return at end

Where to Take the Mock

  • NORCET Excellence Hub free mock tests (Stage 2 simulator)
  • Topic-wise mock bank
  • External: PW / Nurse Guru / Nprep / Vedant Nursing full-length Stage 2 sets

During the Mock — Pacing Targets

  • Q 1–40 → by minute 45
  • Q 41–80 → by minute 90
  • Q 81–120 → by minute 135
  • Q 121–160 → by minute 175
  • Last 5 min: bubble check (if OMR), review flagged items
Skip Rule: If confidence < 60%, mark for review and skip. Never waste >90 sec on a single question on first pass.
5 hours

Error Analysis — The Most Important Part

Step 1: Score & Tally

Net Score = Correct − (Wrong / 3)
Accuracy = Correct / Attempted × 100
  • Target: Net score ≥ 120 / 160 on mock 1
  • If <100, review subject weightage and re-plan Day 12 carefully

Step 2: Classify Every Wrong + Guessed Question

Error TypeFix
Knowledge gapOpen textbook, revise the sub-topic fully, add to error log
Silly mistake (read wrong, calc slip, didn't see "EXCEPT")Write the trap pattern on a dedicated "silly mistakes" sheet
Time pressure (had to guess)Build skip-discipline, drill calculation speed, use microdrip shortcut
Distractor trap (2 options looked equally right)Research all 4 options; note which concept eliminates the wrong 2
Guessed correctlyTreat as weak — revise the concept, solve 5 more MCQs on it

Step 3: Topic-Wise Revision of Wrong Answers

  • Group wrong questions by subject (Med-Surg, Peds, OBG, Fundamentals, etc.)
  • Revise each topic from standard source: Lewis, Brunner & Suddarth, Dutta's OBG, Ghai's Peds, Park's Community Health
  • After reading, attempt 5 fresh MCQs on that topic

Step 4: Build the Mock-1 Insight Sheet

On one page summarize:
  • Total score · accuracy % · subject-wise weak list
  • Top 10 concepts you missed
  • Top 5 traps / negative-marking pitfalls
  • Time-management adjustments for mock 2

End-of-Day Action

Sleep by 10 PM. Do NOT cram new topics after 9 PM. Mind needs consolidation for mock 2 tomorrow.
