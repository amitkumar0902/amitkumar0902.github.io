2026 Updates

  • HPV added to NIS (age 9–14 y girls, 2-dose, 6-mo gap). Indigenous CERVAVAC approved in India.
  • U-WIN — Aadhaar-linked digital immunization registry, nationwide in 2024.
  • Rotavirus — universal coverage achieved across all states by 2022.
  • Pneumococcal — PCV universal by 2021 (replaced by indigenous PCV-14 from 2023).

Cold chain

  • Ice-lined refrigerator (ILR) +2 °C to +8 °C — most vaccines.
  • Deep freezer −15 °C to −25 °C — OPV, measles diluent not frozen.
  • Open-vial policy: OPV/Penta/Td usable up to 28 days if intact and <8 °C; BCG/MR/JE discarded 4 h post-reconstitution.
