BMW 2016 — Color Coding

ColorContentsTreatment
YellowHuman anatomical waste, soiled (blood-soaked cotton/dressings), expired & discarded medicines, chemical liquid waste, microbiology & biotechnology waste, clinical lab waste, animal anatomical wasteIncineration / plasma pyrolysis / deep burial
RedNon-chlorinated plastics: catheters, IV sets, syringes without needles, urine bags, tubing, glovesAutoclaving / microwaving then shredded & recycled
White (translucent)Sharps: needles, scalpels, blades, broken glass (contaminated)Autoclaving / dry heat + encapsulation + shredding
BlueGlassware, metallic body implants (cardboard box with blue tag)Disinfection (1 % sodium hypochlorite) then recycled
BlackGeneral / municipal solid waste (non-hazardous)Municipal solid waste management rules

Key rules

  • Segregate at the point of generation — no mixing.
  • Containers filled up to 3/4 only.
  • Yellow + white sharps go to CBWTF within 48 h.
  • Needle cutters / hub destroyers at source for sharps.
  • Report accidental spills / needle stick injuries immediately.
