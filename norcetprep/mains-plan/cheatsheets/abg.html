ABG — Arterial Blood Gases

Normal values

pH7.35 – 7.45PaO₂80 – 100 mmHg
PaCO₂35 – 45 mmHgSaO₂95 – 100 %
HCO₃⁻22 – 26 mEq/LBase excess−2 to +2

ROME mnemonic

  • Respiratory = Opposite (pH and PaCO₂ move in opposite directions)
  • Metabolic = Equal (pH and HCO₃ move the same way)

4-step method

  1. Look at pH: <7.35 acidosis · >7.45 alkalosis · else normal / fully compensated.
  2. Look at PaCO₂: respiratory cause?
  3. Look at HCO₃⁻: metabolic cause?
  4. Match: pH ↔ PaCO₂ or pH ↔ HCO₃ (ROME). Check compensation.

Anion gap

AG = Na⁺ − (Cl⁻ + HCO₃⁻). Normal 8–12. High-AG metabolic acidosis → MUDPILES: Methanol, Uremia, DKA, Propylene glycol, Isoniazid, Lactic, Ethylene glycol, Salicylate.
