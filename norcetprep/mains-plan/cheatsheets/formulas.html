Clinical Formulas — one-pager

Fluids & burns

  • Parkland: 4 mL × kg × %TBSA over 24 h; half in first 8 h (LR).
  • Modified Brooke: 2 mL × kg × %TBSA.
  • Maintenance fluid (4-2-1): 4 mL/kg first 10 kg + 2 mL/kg next 10 kg + 1 mL/kg thereafter (per hour).
  • Holiday-Segar (24 h): 100-50-20 mL/kg daily equivalent.

Drug calculation

  • D/H × V: Desired dose ÷ Available × Volume.
  • Drip rate = (mL × drop factor) ÷ (time in min) → gtts/min.
  • Microdrip 60 gtts/mL · Macrodrip 10/15/20 gtts/mL.
  • mcg/kg/min: (dose × kg × 60) ÷ concentration (mcg/mL).

Airway sizing

  • ET tube (uncuffed): (age ÷ 4) + 4.
  • ET tube (cuffed): (age ÷ 4) + 3.5.
  • ET depth (cm at lip): internal diameter × 3.
  • Suction catheter Fr: ET ID × 2.

CPR & BLS (AHA adult)

  • Ratio 30:2 · Rate 100–120/min · Depth 5–6 cm · Allow recoil.
  • Pulseless VT/VF: shock + epi 1 mg q3–5min + amiodarone 300 mg then 150 mg.
  • Pediatric 2-rescuer: 15:2.
  • Anaphylaxis: epinephrine 0.3–0.5 mg IM (1:1000) — repeat q5–15 min.

Obstetrics

  • MgSO4 Pritchard: 4 g IV over 20 min + 10 g IM (5 g each buttock); maintenance 5 g IM q4h.
  • Therapeutic Mg level 4–7 mEq/L · Loss of DTR 8–10 · Resp depress >10 · Antidote Ca gluconate 1 g IV.
  • Pre-eclampsia Dx: BP ≥140/90 after 20 wk + proteinuria / end-organ.
