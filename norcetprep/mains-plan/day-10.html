Weak Area Identification & Targeted Revision

How to Identify Weak Areas

  1. Review error log from Days 1–9 practice sets
  2. List topics with accuracy < 75%
  3. Flag topics you answered correctly but guessed (these are fragile)
  4. Re-read textbook section for each weak topic
  5. Immediately attempt 10 fresh MCQs on that topic

Common NORCET Mains Weak Spots (Start Here if Unsure)

  • Drug calculations involving drip rates & mcg/kg/min
  • ABG interpretation with compensation
  • ECG heart block identification
  • Priority / delegation scenarios
  • Obstetric emergencies (cord prolapse, shoulder dystocia, ruptured uterus)
  • Pediatric developmental milestones past 2 years
  • Psychiatric diagnoses & therapies (DBT, CBT, aversion, exposure)
  • Blood transfusion reactions & management
  • Fluid / electrolyte imbalances (Na correction rules)
  • National program year & minister
Reverse-learning technique: For every weak topic, write 5 MCQs yourself (with distractors). Creating questions forces you to spot every detail the examiner might test.

Quick Fix-Up Review — Don't Skip

  • Revise ABG values · ETT formula (Age/4 + 4) · Parkland formula
  • Eclampsia MgSO₄ antidote = Calcium gluconate
  • APGAR score components · NRP 3:1 ratio
  • HPV 2026 single-dose details
  • Venturi colors: Blue-24, White-28, Yellow-35, Red-40, Green-60
  • BMW 2016 color coding (Yellow–Red–White–Blue)
