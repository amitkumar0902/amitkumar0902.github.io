What you get

  • Stage 1: 100 MCQs, 90-minute countdown, 5 sections × 20 questions.

How to start

Go to Mock Tests page from the hub, choose Stage 1 or Stage 2, and begin. Use a quiet environment and disable phone notifications. If the timer reaches zero, the test auto-submits. You can also end early with “Submit full test.”

Limitations (honest)

We are not affiliated with AIIMS. Question distribution is random from our educational bank, not a clone of any official paper. Interface features may differ from the actual test engine. Use mocks for stamina, accuracy, and time discipline — not as a guarantee of exam marks.

Preparation stack

Combine mocks with PYQ-style questions, pharmacology revision, and syllabus mapping. For video learners, search YouTube for “NORCET PYQ” and follow channels you trust; our mock page also links sample playlists.

Who is this for?

Nursing officers and aspirants preparing for AIIMS NORCET (and similar nursing competitive patterns in India) who want browser-based practice without installing apps. If your coaching already gives mocks, use ours as extra volume and speed training.

Exam-day style habits

  • Read the stem once, mark keywords (except, not, first, best).
  • Use section submit only when you are done with that block — same as many CBT flows.
  • Track “marked for review” sparingly; too many marks = no real priority.
  • After each mock, revise wrong + guessed-right topics in the topic modules.
