    Strategy for Top 1–10 AIR

    1. PYQ backbone. Finish every NORCET 6–9 Mains Q. Research every wrong option until you can defend it (1:4 rule).
    2. Zero weak zones. Psych, Micro, and Admin are small but free marks — the dashboard surfaces your weakest topics automatically.
    3. Negative-mark discipline. −1/3 per wrong means guess only when you can eliminate 2 options. Target <10 wrong per mock.
    4. Speed ceiling. 67 s per question average. The drill mode enforces it; build the reflex.
    5. Daily spaced review. Every miss re-appears until answered right twice. Don't skip the "Review due cards" step.
    6. Mock post-mortem > mock count. 10 mocks, 10 hours of analysis each. The mock is the cheap half; the review is where the rank comes from.
