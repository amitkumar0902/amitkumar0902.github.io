NORCET Previous Year Questions — Complete Guide

Previous year questions (PYQs) are the fastest way to understand examiner preferences: topic mix, difficulty, and “trap” options. For AIIMS NORCET, official papers may be released in limited form; many students rely on memory-based compilations from coaching channels, forums, and educators. Always cross-check facts with standard textbooks (e.g., Lewis, Brunner & Suddarth, CHN texts, pharmacology references) because memory-based keys can contain errors.

Where to find official and unofficial PYQs

The official recruitment portal (aiimsexams.ac.in) publishes notifications, sometimes sample papers or instructions. Third-party sites (Prepp, PW, Testbook, etc.) host PDFs and paid test series; free blogs and nursing sites (e.g., The Nurse Page) sometimes publish solved memory-based items. YouTube educators such as Utkarsh Nursing Classes offer playlist walkthroughs of NORCET papers — excellent for learning how to think through options under time pressure.

Study method that works

First pass: attempt a timed block (e.g., 25 questions in 22 minutes) without peeking at answers. Second pass: review every wrong and guessed item; write the concept on a one-page “error log.” Third pass: link the concept to your subject module (e.g., a pharmacology antidote question → revise antidote table in Pharmacology MCQs). Repeat weekly until accuracy stabilizes above your target threshold. For negative marking (−1/3), practice disciplined skipping when confidence is below ~60%.
