## Question

When are the next NORCET cycles, and how many people sit them? Establish: dates
announced or expected for NORCET 11 / NORCET 12 (prelims + mains, per
aiimsexams.ac.in and credible news), the historical cadence (how many cycles per
year, announcement-to-exam lead time), candidate volumes per recent cycle, and
any announced pattern or eligibility changes. NORCET 10 Mains (2026) is the
cycle the site's current Mains toolkit targeted.

Deliverable: a timeline of upcoming cycles + candidate-volume estimates — the
launch-timing constraint that pricing, content-drop cadence, and build
sequencing must respect. Asset file linked from the resolution.

## Resolution

Researched 2026-07-31. NORCET 11 is announced (Notice 103/2026, 24 Jul 2026):
registration open to 13 Aug 2026, Prelims 12 Sep 2026, Mains 30 Sep 2026
(prelims result 18 Sep, mains result 10 Oct). NORCET 12 is not announced;
the locked biannual cadence (April cycle notified late Feb, September cycle
notified late Jul) implies ~Feb 2027 notification, ~mid-Apr 2027 prelims,
~early-May 2027 mains. Lead times: notification→prelims ~6–7 wks;
prelims→mains 18–21 days; prelims-result→mains only 9–14 days. Volumes are
growing: 92,026 appeared in NORCET 10 prelims (vs 62–75k in 2024), but only
8k–19k are shortlisted per Mains, and 1.5k–3.6k posts per cycle (~2.5–4%
selection; repeat attempts the norm). Pattern unchanged for NORCET 11 (Stage
II: 160 Q, 4 locked 45-min sections, −1/3); eligibility unchanged (B.Sc/GNM+
exp, 18–30 gen). Launch constraint: be live before 12 Sep prelims and
certainly before the 18–30 Sep mains window, else demand resumes ~Feb 2027.
