## Question

Map the NORCET / nursing-exam prep market so pricing and differentiation
decisions rest on facts, not vibes. Who are the players an aspirant actually
chooses between (Testbook, Adda247, PrepLadder / Nursing Next Live, NORCET
King–style niche apps, Marrow-style platforms, Telegram content sellers)? For
each: what they charge — especially subscription / validity plans — what the
money buys (mock counts, question-bank size, video, doubt support), and their
app presence (installs, ratings). Where are the exploitable gaps for a lean,
high-quality, web-first product with a strong free funnel?

Deliverable: a comparison table plus a short "gaps we can win" list, as an
asset file. Feeds [Pricing & packaging](t06-pricing-packaging.md) and the
growth fog on the map.
