## Question

Map the NORCET / nursing-exam prep market so pricing and differentiation
decisions rest on facts, not vibes. Who are the players an aspirant actually
chooses between (Testbook, Adda247, PrepLadder / Nursing Next Live, NORCET
King–style niche apps, Marrow-style platforms, Telegram content sellers)? For
each: what they charge — especially subscription / validity plans — what the
money buys (mock counts, question-bank size, video, doubt support), and their
app presence (installs, ratings). Where are the exploitable gaps for a lean,
high-quality, web-first product with a strong free funnel?

Deliverable: a comparison table plus a short "gaps we can win" list, as an
asset file. Feeds [Pricing & packaging](t06-pricing-packaging.md) and the
growth fog on the map.

## Resolution

Market splits into four bands (all prices as of 2026-07-31, promo-volatile):
Band 1 commodity mega-test-series — Adda247 Test Prime ₹299/12 mo, Testbook
Pass ₹399/yr (NORCET series 282 tests but only 25 skill/image tests; nursing
is a thin slice of 500+ exams). Band 2 value batches ₹1.5k–7k — PW Nightingale
₹4,499/cycle, Utkarsh/Neuron ₹2–4k. Band 3 flagship nursing verticals ₹8k–33k —
Nursing Next Live (30k MCQs, 900+ video hrs, plans ₹1,095–₹32,667, 8,100+
selections claim, 100K+ installs) and funded startup NPrep (₹1,527–₹30,767,
40K customers, $1.5M pre-seed). "NORCET King" doesn't exist as a brand; the
niche-app tier is ~77K-install apps. Marrow has no nursing vertical. Telegram
is both the market's free funnel and its piracy engine (video = most pirated
asset; QBank = piracy-resistant). Exploitable gap: the ₹500–₹1,500/yr
NORCET-specialist slot is empty — explanation-first QBank + Mains/Stage-II
depth + transparent pricing + on-product daily free funnel beats both bands.
Suggested anchor for T06: ~₹999/yr headline, ₹149–199/mo, ride the 12 Sep 2026
prelims season. [full findings](../assets/research-competitor-landscape.md)
