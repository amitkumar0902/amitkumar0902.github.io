# Competitor & pricing landscape — NORCET / Indian nursing-exam prep

Research for ticket T01. All prices and offers observed **as of 2026-07-31** unless
noted; this market runs perpetual "sales", so treat every number as a snapshot.
Context anchor: NORCET 11 applications 24 Jul – 13 Aug 2026, Prelims 12 Sep 2026,
Mains late Sep 2026 (Testbook says 30 Sep; Utkarsh says 18 Sep) — peak buying
season is right now through mid-September.

## TL;DR

The market splits into four price bands. Band 1 (₹299–₹649) is commodity
mega-test-series from horizontal giants (Adda247 Test Prime ₹299/12 mo promo,
Testbook Pass ₹399/yr promo) where nursing is one of 500+ exam categories and
per-question quality is weak. Band 3 (₹9k–₹33k) is video-heavy nursing-vertical
coaching (Nursing Next Live, NPrep GOLD, Adda Mahapack, offline-style academies).
Between them sits a mostly empty slot: a **NORCET-specialist, explanation-first
QBank + mock product at ₹500–₹1,500/yr** with real Mains/Stage-II depth. "NORCET
King" as a named app does not meaningfully exist — the niche-app archetype is
NORCET Practice Hub / NURSING 2 NORCET / Nurseasy-scale apps (tens of thousands
of installs). Marrow itself has **no** nursing vertical; nobody yet delivers a
Marrow-grade QBank experience for nursing. That, plus a trust-first free funnel
(the thing incumbents outsource to Telegram/YouTube), is the wedge.

## Player profiles

### Testbook (horizontal giant)
- **App**: "Testbook: Exam Preparation App" — 50M+ Play installs; iOS app exists.
- **Pricing** (2026-07-31): unified **Testbook Pass ₹399/365 days** (67% off MRP
  ₹1,199; former Pass/Pro/Max/Elite tiers merged). **SuperCoaching "Super Pass
  Live"** for NORCET: ₹1 for a 2-day trial, then **₹649 per 90 days, recurring**
  (a ₹599/90d variant was also observed — price tests in flight).
- **What money buys (NORCET)**: NORCET-11 test series of **282 tests** — 12 full
  mocks, 19 previous-year papers, 100 nursing chapter tests, 40 nursing subject
  tests, only **5 skill-assessment + 20 image-based tests**, 86 non-tech tests;
  2 free tests as funnel. Pass also unlocks 1.5L+ mocks / 30k+ PYPs across
  1,000+ exams, doubt support.
- **Read**: unbeatable on volume-per-rupee; weak on nursing-specific depth,
  Mains/skill content, and explanation quality. Recurring-billing pattern breeds
  distrust (visible in reviews culture).

### Adda247 (horizontal giant)
- **App**: 4 crore+ students claimed, 4.4★, ~90 lakh reviews (their claims).
- **Pricing** (2026-07-31): **Test Prime ₹299/12 months** (perpetual "month-end
  sale"; coupon PRIME70), unlimited re-attempts, "500% refund on selection"
  gimmick. **Nursing Mahapack** (live coaching bundle): listed **₹13,699/12 mo,
  ₹18,199/18 mo, ₹22,799/27 mo**; individual nursing video courses ₹2,271–₹4,000.
- **What money buys (NORCET)**: NORCET series inside Test Prime — currently
  **101 tests** (87 exam + 14 chapter); an earlier NORCET-10 series advertised
  202 mocks. Mahapack: 10,000 hrs live classes, 15,123 videos, 1,674 mocks,
  30 e-books across 9 nursing exams (NORCET, RRB, CHO, AIIMS CRE, state staff
  nurse…). 3 free tests as funnel.
- **Read**: owns the ₹299 price anchor for "all the mocks you could ever want."
  Quality reputation is mixed (translation artifacts, repeated questions). Their
  nursing YouTube/Telegram arms feed the funnel.

### Physics Wallah (value-brand disruptor)
- **Pricing** (2026-07-31): **Nightingale Regular for AIIMS NORCET 11 —
  ₹4,499 early bird**, validity to 31 Oct 2026 (one-cycle validity, not 12 mo);
  Nightingale Exam Booster ₹1,799; **Nursing Pro 2.0 (all nursing exams)
  ₹6,999**, Jun 2026–May 2027. 21 nursing batches live.
- **What money buys**: 600+ lectures, 500+ DPPs, 5,000+ practice questions,
  100+ mock tests, "Nurse Pedia" notes, 3D-model teaching, Hinglish medium.
- **Read**: the trusted-brand budget coach. Video-first; their mock/QBank layer
  is comparatively thin (5k questions vs NNL's 30k). One-cycle validity means
  repeat purchase each attempt.

### Nursing Next Live / NNL ONE (the vertical leader — "Marrow of nursing" in ambition)
- **App**: NNL ONE (new) + legacy "Nursing Next Live" — 100K+ downloads,
  4.3–4.7★ depending on source; claims 2,00,000+ learners, **8,100+ NORCET
  selections 2020–2026**, AIR 1/2/5/6/8/9 in recent NORCETs, "80% strike rate."
- **Pricing** (observed): plans span **₹1,095 to ₹32,667** (third-party
  comparison, 2025); **Fastrack 10.0 + Strike 2.0 test-series pack ₹9,999 → ₹8,999**
  (own site, 2026-07). Flagship **Plan C+ X / Platinum Edition** (recorded):
  12–24 months, 900+ video hours, 20,000+ MCQs, Masterstroke books. **Plan MLB
  Pro** (live; Red/Blue Sapphire batches) is the premium live tier. Prices are
  deliberately hidden behind app/login — high-touch sales motion.
- **What money buys**: 30,000+ MCQ smart QBank, 1,200+ learning hours, CBT-style
  test series, mentorship, offline NNL Academy centres.
- **Read**: the aspirational default for serious full-time aspirants; expensive,
  video-heavy, Hinglish. Its result-marketing (toppers, strike rate) is the moat.
  Piracy of its lectures on Telegram is rampant — which punishes video, not QBank.

### NPrep (funded vertical startup)
- **Company**: Jodhpur, founded 2023; **$1.5M pre-seed (Lumikai, All In
  Capital)**; ~40K customers / 26K active students (Aug 2025); Play + App Store.
- **Pricing**: **₹1,527 to ₹30,767** by module (BSc college courses → NORCET
  packs); example: BSc 1st-year plan ₹7,799 (MRP ₹11,999). QBank + Test Series
  product: **30,000+ MCQs**, daily tests, 100+ PYQs, unlimited reattempts;
  Rapid Revision 100-hr crash; NPrep GOLD full-curriculum flagship.
- **Read**: closest strategic analogue to us (QBank-led, AI-personalization
  positioning, claims 215 NORCET-2024 selections) but has gone video+college-
  curriculum heavy and priced into Band 3. App-first, not web-first.

### Marrow (check: does the archetype exist in nursing?)
- **No.** Marrow (DailyRounds/M3) is NEET-PG/NExT only — Plan C ≈ ₹28,500/yr for
  PG medicos. No NORCET/nursing vertical found. The "Marrow-grade QBank
  experience for nursing" slot is genuinely unoccupied — NNL and NPrep approximate
  it but both lead with video hours, not explanation quality.

### Niche "NORCET King"-style apps & long tail
- **"NORCET King" itself: not found** as a significant app/brand on Play Store or
  web — treat it as shorthand for this archetype.
- NURSING 2 NORCET (Education Mark Media): ~77K downloads, ~96/day — lectures +
  quizzes + simulated exams.
- NORCET Practice Hub: topic practice, NORCET-pattern mocks, PYQ-style questions,
  "AI-based guidance" — small installs.
- Nurseasy (NUREASY): Hinglish lectures ("Dr. Marwah"), Plan C product,
  YouTube-live + Telegram-quiz + Instagram-flashcard funnel; claims rankers from
  remote-area self-study users.
- Neuron Nursing Academy (Classplus-style store): NORCET 11+12 live batch
  **₹3,999** (MRP ₹12,999), all-exams pack ₹4,499, test+material pack ₹1,999,
  **free weekly mocks + daily tests** as funnel.
- Budget test-series sellers: TestKart ₹299 (Stage-II series), Closecampus ₹370
  (Mains mock), Toppersexam/EduRev free-to-cheap tests.

### Telegram ecosystem (two distinct roles)
1. **Free-funnel channels** — daily MCQs, PDFs, job alerts (VS Nursing Academy,
   Nurses Class, RNCC, Nurseasy's quiz channel). This is where aspirants live
   between purchases; every serious player runs one.
2. **Piracy resellers** — leaked NNL/PW/academy lectures and PDFs at throwaway
   prices or free; Indian coaching piracy losses estimated around ₹2,000 crore
   with thousands of groups. Consequence: **video courses are the most pirated,
   least defensible asset; a continuously updated server-side QBank with
   accounts/analytics is far more piracy-resistant.**

## Gaps we can win (lean, high-quality, web-first, strong free funnel)

1. **The ₹500–₹1,500/yr specialist slot is empty.** Band 1 is generic volume;
   Band 3 is expensive video. A NORCET-specialist QBank + mock subscription at
   ~₹999/yr undercuts NNL/NPrep by 10–20x while being visibly better than
   Adda/Testbook on nursing depth. Nobody credible occupies this today.
2. **Mains / Stage-II depth.** Testbook has only 5 skill + 20 image tests; most
   players sell prelims-style MCQ volume. Our Mains toolkit (per-paper PYQ
   mocks, labelled diagrams, toppers analysis) is already ahead of the field —
   make it the paid centerpiece.
3. **Explanation quality as the product.** Incumbent explanations are thin,
   translated, or missing. AI-drafted + engineer-reviewed rationales with
   diagrams, done consistently across ~10k questions, is a Marrow-grade
   experience nursing has never had — and text is cheap for us to produce.
4. **No-video economics + piracy resistance.** Video is the incumbents' cost
   anchor and their most pirated asset. A QBank-first product loses little to
   Telegram piracy and needs no studio spend.
5. **Web-first speed and trust.** Incumbents are app-first, login-walled,
   notification-spammy, with fake-MRP countdown sales and recurring-billing
   traps (Super Pass Live). A fast PWA, generous free tier (547 MCQs + daily
   quiz), transparent flat pricing, no dark patterns — trust is a feature this
   market conspicuously lacks. The Play Store wrapper covers discovery.
6. **Own the free funnel on-product.** The market's funnel runs on Telegram/
   YouTube (daily MCQs, PDFs). Put the daily free quiz, PYQ-of-the-day, and
   streaks on our own site/app so the funnel converts where the paywall is,
   with Telegram/YouTube as feeders, not homes.
7. **Cutoff-anchored analytics.** Percentile vs actual NORCET stage cutoffs and
   weak-topic maps beat the generic "all-India rank" reports of Band-1 giants —
   cheap to build on our own mock data.
8. **Multi-exam extensibility on one QBank.** Every incumbent bundles RRB/ESIC/
   CHO/state staff-nurse exams; tagging our bank per exam replicates that at
   near-zero marginal cost, matching the locked "NORCET-first, extensible" plan.
9. **English-first + Hinglish option.** Vertical incumbents are Hinglish-first;
   English-medium aspirants (esp. South India, NCLEX-track nurses) are
   underserved.
10. **Season timing.** Buying peaks Jul–Sep (NORCET window). Launch pricing and
    the mock-heavy upsell should ride the 12 Sep 2026 prelims; one-cycle
    validity products (PW) force rebuys — our 12-month validity is a talking
    point.

### Implications for T06 (pricing & packaging)
- Free tier must beat Adda's ₹299 on *quality and trust*, not volume.
- Headline annual ≈ ₹999/yr sits above Testbook Pass ₹399 (avoids
  commodity-price association) and far below NNL/NPrep flagships (avoids
  price-anchored comparison); a monthly at ₹149–199 catches exam-season buyers.
- Avoid: recurring-billing traps, fake MRPs, countdown timers. Do: simple
  flat price, visible sample explanations, refund honesty.

### Caveats
- Every price above is promo-volatile; Adda's ₹299 and Testbook's ₹399 are
  "perpetual sales" that occasionally revert to MRP.
- NNL's flagship pricing is intentionally opaque; the ₹1,095–₹32,667 range comes
  from a competitor's 2025 comparison plus NNL's own ₹8,999 test-series page.
- Install/user counts mix store data and self-claims; treat as order-of-magnitude.
