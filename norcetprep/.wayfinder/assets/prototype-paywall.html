Daily free quiz · Q4/10 · Pharmacology

A patient on IV heparin develops a platelet count of 40,000/mm³ on day 5. The nurse should anticipate which immediate order?
Why B: A ≥50% platelet fall around day 4–10 of heparin suggests heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT), a prothrombotic emergency — all heparin (including LMWH) is stopped and a direct thrombin inhibitor (e.g., argatroban) started. Platelet transfusion is avoided; dose reduction does not stop immune platelet activation.
Source: Brunner & Suddarth 15e, Ch. 33 · cross-checked, human-reviewed

How our questions are checked

NurseDrill is built openly by an engineer — here's exactly what stands behind every question:

1 · Consistency checks. Every item passes automated validation — schema, options, answer keys, duplicates — on every change.
2 · Independent cross-verification. Each question and its explanation is challenged by a separate verification pass; anything it can't confirm is flagged.
3 · Human review. All flagged items and a random sample of everything else are reviewed by hand before release.
4 · Sources. Every question cites a standard nursing reference.
5 · Public fixes. Every reported error and its fix is published, dated, in the fix-log.

A weekly mock that hasn't finished review ships late — never unreviewed.
