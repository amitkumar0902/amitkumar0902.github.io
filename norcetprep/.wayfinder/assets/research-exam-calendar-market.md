Researched 2026-07-31. All "announced" facts verified against the NORCET 11
notification coverage and official AIIMS documents where available; volatile

## Headline

- NORCET 11 is announced (Notice No. 103/2026, released 24 Jul 2026):
  **Prelims 12 Sep 2026, Mains 30 Sep 2026**. Registration open now
  (24 Jul – 13 Aug 2026, 5 PM).
- NORCET 12 is **not announced** as of 2026-07-31. By the stable biannual
  rhythm, expect notification ~Feb 2027, Prelims ~mid-Apr 2027, Mains
  ~early-May 2027.
- Cadence is a locked two-per-year pattern since 2024: an **April cycle**
  (notification late Feb) and a **September cycle** (notification late
  Jul/early Aug). Notification-to-prelims lead is ~6–7 weeks; prelims-to-mains
  gap is ~2.5–3 weeks; results land within days.
- Per-cycle market: **~90–100k registrations / ~92k appeared** at prelims
  (NORCET 10), of whom only **~14–19k are shortlisted for Mains**. Mains
  window (prelims result → mains exam) is **~12–14 days**.

## Historical cadence, NORCET 5 → 11

Two-stage format (qualifying Prelims + merit-deciding Mains) began with
NORCET 5. NORCET 1–4 (2020 – Jun 2023) were single-exam cycles.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NORCET 5 | Advt No. 146/2023 dated 05 Aug 2023 | 17 Sep 2023 | 7 Oct 2023 | Oct 2023 (9,140 qualified) | 3,055 |
| NORCET 6 | Reg 26 Feb – 17 Mar 2024 | 14 Apr 2024 | 5 May 2024 | May 2024 | not disclosed (est. 3.5–4k) |
| NORCET 7 | Reg begins Aug 2024 | 15 Sep 2024 | 4 Oct 2024 | 16 Oct 2024 | 1,487 |

| NORCET 11 | Notice 103/2026, 24 Jul 2026; reg to 13 Aug | 12 Sep 2026 | 30 Sep 2026 | 10 Oct 2026 (sched.) | 2,218 (tentative) |

Lead-time facts derived from the table:

- Notification → Prelims: ~6–7 weeks (N5: 6.1 wks; N11: 7.1 wks).
- Prelims → Mains: 18–21 days every cycle since N6 (N11: 18 days).
- Prelims result → Mains: **9–14 days** (N11: 12 days) — the entire
  high-intent Mains-prep purchase window.
- Mains → result: 4–12 days; seat allocation single online round, **no
  waiting list**; vacant seats roll to the next cycle (official NORCET-5
  brochure) — which is why vacancy counts oscillate (1.5k–3.6k).

Reading of the numbers:

- **Prelims TAM is growing**: 62–75k appeared per cycle in 2024 → 92k in
  Apr 2026 (+35% over two years). Two cycles/year gives ~150–190k
  exam-sittings/year, but with heavy repeat overlap (many candidates sit
  both cycles), unique annual candidates are plausibly ~100–130k.
- **Mains audience is small and time-boxed**: 8k–19k shortlisted per cycle,
  known only ~2 weeks before the Mains exam. A Mains-only product has a
  narrow, spiky demand curve; a Prelims+Mains product addresses the full
  ~90k funnel from notification day.
- **Selection ratio**: ~2.2–3.6k posts vs ~90k aspirants → ~2.5–4%
  selection; ~60% of the shortlisted Mains cohort "qualifies" but only
  vacancy-count get seats. Repeat attempts are the norm — favorable for
  subscriptions spanning multiple cycles.
- Application fee ~Rs 3,000 (Gen/OBC; NORCET 10 reporting) — candidates
  already spend meaningfully per attempt; anchors paid-product pricing.

## Pattern & eligibility (current, with change history)

- **Two-stage format since NORCET 5 (Sep 2023)**: Stage I qualifying only;
  Stage II alone determines merit ("NORCET Rank").
- **Stage I** (per NORCET 11 coverage): 100 MCQs / 90 min — 80 nursing +
  20 non-nursing, reported as 5 timed sections of 18 min each.
- **Stage II**: 160 MCQs / 180 min in **4 locked sections of 40 Q × 45 min**
  (no going back between sections); +1 correct, −1/3 wrong. Heavy
  clinical/case-scenario orientation. This is the format the site's Mains
  toolkit targets; NORCET 11 keeps it — **no pattern change announced**
  for NORCET 11 (checked 2026-07-31).
- **Mains shortlisting** tightened over time: percentile-qualifiers
  (35–51k) vs actually-called-for-Mains (8–19k). Cutoff percentiles
  NORCET 10 (UR 93.59, OBC 84.21, SC 81.96, EWS 78.98, ST 74.47).
- **Eligibility (NORCET 11)**: B.Sc (Hons)/B.Sc Nursing/Post-Basic B.Sc, or
  GNM diploma + registered nurse & midwife + experience (reported 2 yrs;
  official docs require experience certificates stating hospital bed count),
  M.Sc accepted per some summaries. **Age 18–30** (general) as on 13 Aug
  2026; +3 OBC, +5 SC/ST, +10 PwBD. No eligibility change vs prior cycles.

## Launch-timing implications (the constraint T02 exists to establish)

1. **NORCET 11 is the live cycle**: registration is open now (to 13 Aug
   2026), prelims in ~6 weeks (12 Sep), mains in ~9 weeks (30 Sep). A paid
   launch that misses 30 Sep 2026 has no meaningful demand until the
   NORCET 12 ramp (~Feb 2027 notification).
2. Demand comes in two spikes per cycle: notification→prelims (~90k-wide,
   6–7 weeks) and prelims-result→mains (~14–19k, ~12 days, highest intent —
   this is where the existing Mains toolkit fits).
3. Content-drop cadence should mirror the biannual calendar: refresh/PYQ
   drops at each notification (late Feb / late Jul), mains-sprint drops at
   each prelims result (mid-Apr / mid-Sep).
4. Pricing horizon: repeat attempts across cycles argue for a 6-month
   (one-cycle) and 12-month (two-cycle) subscription split rather than
   lifetime access.
