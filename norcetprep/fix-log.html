Every error we've corrected

A live question bank has errors. The honest measures are how fast they get found and whether the fixes are visible — so every correction lands here, dated, including the ones that are embarrassing to publish. Reports are triaged weekly; fixes ship with the weekly publish and appear here the same day.

Found something wrong?

Every question in the study app has a report control — two taps, pick a reason. That is the fastest route, and it is the one that ends up on this page. Reporting is not a doubt-solving channel; for how questions are built and checked in the first place, see the methodology page.
